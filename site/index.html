WHAT HAPPENSThe table fights back

WHAT'S INSIDEA run, three floors deep

45 RELICS

Explosive chalk, balls with orbits, pockets that get hungry. Pick one you already own and it upgrades.

6 BOSSES

A table that tilts, pockets that spit, a pocket that eats shots, a Dealer, a Clock, and The House.

DAILY SCRATCH

The same seeded run for everyone, every day. Win once to unlock Endless and five BREAK levels.

FAIR SAVES

Close the tab mid-run and pick it up later. Reloading never gets you a free retry.

HIDDEN INSIDEA completely normal game of 8-ball

Press PLAY NORMAL 8-BALL and the tape drags, the CRT clicks off, and you're in a quiet late-night lounge with a jazz trio playing.

Play standard 8-ball against four AI levels that plan with the same physics you play with and never cheat. There's also local two-player on one mouse and a practice mode.

HOW TO PLAYControls

AIMMove the mouse. The dots show where the cue ball goes.
SHOOTHold the left button to charge, then release. The mouse wheel sets the power cap.
SPINW A S D, or drag the dot on the cue-ball widget.
CAMERARight-drag to orbit, Q / E to zoom, C for top-down.
ITEMS1, 2 or 3.
PAUSEEsc, for settings, fullscreen and the switch to Classic.
