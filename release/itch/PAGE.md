**Pool was normal until the table started fighting back.**

SCRATCH is a billiards roguelite that looks like a lost PlayStation disc from 1998. Sink balls, collect relics that bend the rules of pool, and work your way down through three floors of increasingly strange tables to face The House.

It plays in your browser. A full run takes around 30 to 45 minutes, and you can close the tab mid-run and pick it up later.

### How a run works

- **Clear tables** to earn chips and pick one of three relics.
- **Choose your road** at each fork: an event, a mystery, a shop, or the back room.
- **Lose a table** and it costs you a heart. Lose them all and the run is over.
- **Beat the boss** at the bottom of each floor to go deeper.

### What's in it

- **45 relics** that change how the table behaves. Chalk that explodes. Balls with orbits. Pockets that are hungry. Picking one you already own upgrades it.
- **Six bosses:**
  - The Crooked Table tilts.
  - The Mimic's pockets move, and one spits.
  - The Void swallows shots.
  - The Dealer marks a pocket gold and others bust.
  - The Clock takes your shot for you when time runs out.
  - The House plays back against you.
- **High Stakes tables:** one rule you must not break, for a much bigger purse.
- **Rare anomalies** such as a mirrored table, a giant ball, a storm, or zero friction. There is at least one we don't list.
- **Daily Scratch:** the same seeded run for everyone, once a day.
- **Endless**, unlocked by winning, and **BREAK** levels 1 to 5 for harder runs.
- **36 achievements**, unlockable ball sets, cues and club themes, and a collection to fill.

### And a normal game of pool

Hidden in the main menu is **SCRATCH Classic**: a quiet, modern game of standard 8-ball in a late-night lounge, with a jazz trio playing.
- **VS AI** at four levels. The AI plays the same physics you do and never cheats.
- **Local two-player** on one mouse.
- **Practice mode.**

### Controls

- **Aim:** mouse
- **Shoot:** hold and release the left button
- **Spin:** W A S D, or drag the dot on the cue-ball widget
- **Power cap:** mouse wheel
- **Orbit the camera:** right-drag
- **Top-down view:** C
- **Use items:** 1 2 3
- **Pause:** Esc
- **Menus:** mouse, or the arrow keys and Enter

### Options

- **Graphics:** PS1 or Modern, with Low / Medium / High presets.
- **Aim guide:** Full, Short or Off.
- **Camera shake and screen flash:** can be reduced or turned off.
- **UI scale.**
- **Saves:** kept in your browser. They can be exported and imported as a line of text.
